# Zi An Lee — Neo-Roman Museum Portfolio

## Creative direction

- Format: cinematic, scroll-scrubbed continuous forward walkthrough through an artificial clay world
- Budget: Starter / 200 credits
- Architecture: four-scene forward take using Seedance 2.0 Mini at 720p
- Mobile: crop-safe composition; no additional generation credits
- Art direction: handcrafted claymation diorama, tactile fingerprints, rounded forms, warm macro lighting, shallow depth of field
- Character: a recognizable but deliberately stylized clay version of Zi An, never photorealistic
- Tone: premium, charming, intelligent and mysterious
- Palette: parchment `#F2E7D6`, charcoal `#252738`, ochre `#C58B45`, terracotta `#9E4B3F`, lamp amber `#F4B84A`, blue-grey `#6E7890`
